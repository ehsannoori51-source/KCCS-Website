Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about KCCS cold storage services.

What temperature ranges do you offer?+

We operate four chamber types: Fruit & Veg (+4°C), Pharmaceutical (−18°C), Deep Freeze (−25°C), and Ambient Cool (+12°C). Custom temperature settings are available for large-volume clients on request.

What is the minimum storage period?+

Our standard minimum is one month, but we also offer weekly rates for short-term clients such as event caterers, exporters awaiting shipment windows, or emergency pharmaceutical storage needs.

Do you offer delivery and distribution?+

Yes. We provide refrigerated last-mile delivery within Kabul and can arrange inter-provincial logistics through our partner network. Same-day delivery is available for urgent shipments with prior arrangement.

Is your facility safe for pharmaceutical storage?+

Absolutely. Our pharmaceutical chambers meet WHO and international cold chain standards. We maintain continuous temperature logging, backup power systems, and provide documentation suitable for regulatory compliance.

What happens during a power outage?+

We operate dual backup generator systems that activate automatically within seconds of any grid failure. Our facility has never experienced a temperature breach due to power loss, and all events are logged with timestamps.

How do I get started or receive a quote?+

Use our instant Quote Calculator above for an immediate estimate, or fill out the Contact form below and our team will reach out within a few hours. You can also call or WhatsApp us directly for urgent inquiries.

Do you serve clients outside Kabul?+

Our main facility is in Kabul's Industrial Park, but we work with clients from across Afghanistan, including NGOs operating in remote provinces that require secure pharmaceutical cold storage and distribution.

Can I visit the facility before signing?+

Yes, we welcome facility tours by appointment. Simply contact us to schedule a visit and our operations team will walk you through all chambers, monitoring systems, and logistics processes.
